Why "Kairo"

There's clock time. Then there's the moment.

The ancient Greeks had two words for time. Our name comes from the one that matters when a decision is on the line.

χρόνος · chronos
Chronos

Sequential, ticking time. The follow-ups, deadlines and paperwork that fill a day without moving anything forward.

Kairo's AI handles this.

καιρός · kairos
Kairos

The exact, opportune moment for action. The call before a seller lists. The price drop a buyer was waiting for. The offer that lands first.

Your Kairo agent owns this.

In Christian theology, kairos is God's appointed time: a decisive moment when transformation happens. Buying or selling a home is one of those moments. We treat it that way.
